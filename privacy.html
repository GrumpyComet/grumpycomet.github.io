Privacy Policy

Applies to Gatefall and Boom & Bust: Crash Tycoon. Last updated November 22, 2026.

Grumpy Comet Games makes casual mobile games. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we never sell your data.

What the games store

  • Game progress and settings (levels, coins, diamonds, your city and so on) are saved on your device so you can keep playing.
  • Cloud backup (if available). To let you restore progress, a game may back up that save to our server under a random player ID that is created on your device. The ID is not linked to your name or Google account.
  • Gameplay statistics. We may collect anonymous gameplay events (for example, which level was won or lost, or which crash happened) under the same random ID, to balance and improve the games.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases and restore them on a new device; it receives a purchase record and an anonymous app user ID.

Ads

The games show ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and basic device and usage information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or turn off ad personalization in your phone's settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Reminders

Boom & Bust can send local reminders (for example, "a crash is coming"). They are scheduled on your device and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Each game has a Delete my data button in its settings. It erases your progress on the device and asks our server to delete anything stored under your player ID. Uninstalling a game also removes its data from the device. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The games are made for a general audience aged 13 and up and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud saves and gameplay statistics are kept while you play and deleted on request; inactive player data may be deleted after 24 months.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
